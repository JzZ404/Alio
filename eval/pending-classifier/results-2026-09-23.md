## The only miss

One of the eight labels flagged as arguable before the run: information, or
"so go and get it"? The model read it the way the note said someone reasonably
could. This is a disagreement about the label, not a failure to understand the
message.

It is also the single medication miss, which is why that figure is 7/8 rather
than 8/8.

## What this does and does not tell us

**Does:** every request that plainly looks like one was caught, and — the part
worth having — so was every request that *doesn't* look like one. *"Her pills
run out Thursday"*, *"Her walker is still in the car"*, *"The smoke alarm has
been chirping"*, *"Nobody has changed the bed since Sunday"*. The regex stub
misses all four. The traps in the other direction held too: *"Can you believe
she's still doing the crossword?"* and all six past-tense messages came back
`fyi` or `social`.

**Does not:** these are 63 messages written by one person imagining what a
family says, not 63 messages a family actually sent. They are clean, complete
sentences with correct spelling and no shared context. Real messages are
fragments, typos, replies to something three messages earlier. A score on
invented data is evidence the approach works, not evidence of how it will
perform.

The honest summary: good enough to wire in, not good enough to quote as a
number to anyone outside the team.
